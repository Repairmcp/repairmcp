The short version

RepairMCP is a free lookup service over publicly published collision repair data. It is provided as is, with no warranty. It has no accounts, sets no cookies, builds no profiles, and sells nothing. Your estimates and negotiations are your decisions; verify anything that matters against the original inquiry, which is why every answer carries the inquiry number and the date.

What this service is

RepairMCP lets an AI assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT search collision repair reference data that is already published free to the public. Today that is the inquiry database from the Database Enhancement Gateway at degweb.org, vehicle recall, complaint, and identification data served live from the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's public services, the federal vehicle safety statute from the United States Code, and Washington state statutes and regulations captured word for word from the state legislature's public website at leg.wa.gov, Montana statutes and administrative rules captured word for word from the official Montana Code Annotated at mca.legmt.gov and the Secretary of State's rules service at rules.mt.gov (the Montana Legislature notes that the printed code prevails over its web version), and Colorado statutes, regulations, and Division of Insurance guidance captured word for word from the Office of Legislative Legal Services at olls.info, the Secretary of State's rules service at coloradosos.gov, and the Colorado Division of Insurance at doi.colorado.gov (Division of Insurance bulletins are guidance, not law). Privacy

This site

This site sets no cookies, runs no scripts, carries no analytics, and loads nothing from third parties. Nothing about your visit is tracked by the site itself.

The lookup service

There are no accounts and no sign-in. When your AI assistant runs a search, the request reaches a server hosted on Cloudflare. Standard, short-lived operational logs exist there, covering the network address, the time, and what was requested, and they are used only to keep the service healthy and enforce rate limits. No profiles are built, nothing is sold, and there is no advertising.

Your conversations

Your conversation with Claude or ChatGPT happens inside those products, under their own privacy terms. RepairMCP only ever receives the search terms your assistant sends it. It never sees your conversation, your estimates, your files, or your customer information, because it was never given a way to.

If you ask for a recall check by VIN, that VIN is passed straight through to the federal government's own vehicle lookup at NHTSA to identify the year, make, and model. It is not stored, it is never used as a cache key, and only its last six characters ever appear in an answer.

Hosting

The service is delivered through Cloudflare, whose systems process network traffic to serve and protect it under their own terms.
